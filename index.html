FOR MY FAVOURITE HUMAN10 · 10 · ALWAYS
✧

To my best friend,

First of all, happy birthday. I hope today reminds you how special you are, not just to the people around you, but simply as the person you are. You deserve the kind of happiness that feels peaceful, the kind of love that feels safe, and a year filled with little things that make you genuinely smile.

I’m so grateful that our paths crossed. Since 26 August 2025, you’ve become part of a chapter of my life that I want to remember with a full heart. Thank you for the conversations, the laughs, the little moments, and for being someone I get to call my best friend. Even ordinary days can become memories worth keeping when you’re part of them.

There’s also something I want to say honestly. I’m sorry for every moment I may have hurt you — through words I should have chosen more carefully, misunderstandings, mistakes, or times when I didn’t understand what you were feeling. I know that having good intentions doesn’t erase the impact of something hurtful. If I made you feel unheard, unappreciated, or upset, I’m genuinely sorry.

I can’t promise I’ll never make a mistake, but I can promise to keep learning. I want to listen more carefully, communicate more kindly, understand your feelings instead of making assumptions, and take responsibility when I get things wrong. Your feelings matter, and you never owe me instant forgiveness. Take whatever time you need; I respect that.

More than anything, I hope we get to make many more memories — more laughter, spontaneous adventures, silly conversations, honest talks, and moments we’ll look back on years from now. I hope this next year brings you beautiful surprises, growth, comfort, and every good thing your heart has been hoping for.

Thank you for being you, and for being part of my story. I’m really glad our story began.

Happy Birthday, my favourite human. Thank you for being a part of my story. I'm sorry for the moments I hurt you, and I'll keep trying to be a better friend. Here's to the memories we've made and all the beautiful ones still waiting for us.

With all my love,
Your bestie ♡
